# Powerups for gameplay

wide PAD
# Widen pad by '1'

multiply BALL
# every time ball hists rect with more than 1 hit point (balls * 2) spawns from the hit location.
# Each new ball has the opposite direction of the previous ball.
# Lasts 10 seconds

bomb RECT
# When the rect is hit it will explode, doing 1 hit worth of damage the rects to the top, bottom, left, and right of it

stick PAD
# if ball lands on pad, it is stuck to pad until left click to launch ball.

life PAD
# adds 1 extra life.

fast BALL
# makes ball increase speed each bounce. Effects all live balls.
# A ball still in play keeps its speed when another is lost. Ends when no ball is left.


bonus BALL
# applies `score * score` to the level score.
